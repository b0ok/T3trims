About Us

Welcome to T3Trims, the go-to barbershop in Mississauga for top-tier grooming. We specialize in modern fades, beard trims, and personalized haircuts tailored to your style. With years of experience, I ensure precision and attention to detail in every cut. Whether you're looking for a classic look, a sharp fade, or a full grooming experience, we've got you covered. Conveniently located in Mississauga, we offer both in-shop and mobile VIP services for those who prefer the luxury of getting groomed at home.
