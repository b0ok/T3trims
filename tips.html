Grooming Tips

Hair

Don't Overwash

Washing your hair every day strips natural oils. Stick to 2-3x a week for healthier hair.

Less Product is More

A dime-size amount of pomade or clay goes a long way — work it in starting from the back.

Stay on a Fade Schedule

Get your fade touched up every 2-3 weeks to keep the line sharp and the grow-out clean.

Towel Dry, Don't Rub

Rubbing wet hair with a towel roughs up the cuticle and causes frizz. Pat and squeeze instead.

Beard

Beard Maintenance

Trim the edges weekly and use a beard oil to keep it soft, itch-free, and healthy.

Brush It Out Daily

Brushing trains the hair to lay flat and evenly distributes oil, keeping the beard looking fuller and neater.

Keep the Lines Symmetrical

Check both sides in good lighting before you're done trimming — uneven lines are the #1 giveaway of a rushed beard trim.

Facial / Skin

Prep Before You Shave

Exfoliate and use a warm towel before shaving to avoid razor bumps and ingrown hairs.

Moisturize After Every Shave

Shaving strips moisture from the skin — follow up with a fragrance-free moisturizer to prevent dryness and irritation.

Wash Your Face Twice a Day

A gentle cleanser morning and night keeps buildup and razor bumps down, especially if you shave or trim often.
